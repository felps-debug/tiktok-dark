# 01 — Reference analysis
## Role
Analyze the reference as evidence. Do not adapt the story yet.
## Input and reads
Reference URL/local media; requested scope; actual installed Watch guidance.
## Process
1. Verify Watch capability. Use it if available; otherwise state the missing capability and accept accessible video, transcript or frames.
2. Preserve source URL, duration, transcript and evidence index in referencia-original/.
3. Inspect timestamped frames across the whole video, emphasizing cuts and turns. Report inspected/available coverage and missing audio.
4. Identify characters, visible distinguishing traits, environments, exact dialogue where supported, scene sequence, camera/editing and sound only when actually heard.
5. Build beats with time range, literal event, narrative function, emotion, open question and payoff. Distinguish observation from inference.
6. Explain hook mechanism, causal chain, escalation, pacing and ending. Do not assert retention performance without metrics.
7. Analyze audiovisual humor and emotional delivery at actual timestamps: facial changes, eyes/eyebrows/mouth, posture, gestures, scale contrast, props, framing, cuts, pauses, reaction timing, audible voice pitch/pace/volume/emphasis, breathing, sound effects, music and intentional silence. Explain how each cue makes a beat funny or communicates an emotion. Do not infer heard tone from a transcript or label unheard audio as observed.
8. Identify setup/payoff and the precise visual or sonic contrast; separate observed performance from a proposed adaptation. Include non-humorous emotional cues too. Missing audio must be an explicit limitation.
## Output
analysis.md following templates/reference-analysis.md; transcript.md and evidence.json with actual source paths.
## Stop and check
Deliver analysis and limitations. Do not choose an adaptation or generate characters/scenes.
Names inferred from faulty transcription remain uncertain until verified.
